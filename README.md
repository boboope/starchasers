# QuadRun

Enso's board game, built from his drawing. Open the site, pick a name and a color (or draw your own doodle), and play on one phone, against Robo-Chicken (beat all three robots to wake up MEGA-CLUCK), or on two phones with a room password (enso1 to enso5).

Four corners, four guardians: Chicken-Heart, Fire, Electricity and Forest. Win a corner's duel to wear its aura. Psst: the Chicken-Heart guardian loves hearts.

Everything is in `index.html`. Music credits are in `CREDITS.md`.
