STARCHASERS

a game made up by Enso

How to play

1.Everyone starts in the middle. Pick a road, then SWIPE the dice to throw it. Roll a 6, roll again.
2.Land EXACTLY on a door (the slanted tags at the alley ends) to knock. Do the math in 21 seconds and you own that corner and its 2 keys. Owned by your friend? Solve it to STEAL the corner and draw a mustache on their face. Answer fast: leftover seconds give you more ink and more drawing time.
3.Once every corner has an owner, a steal becomes math + an EMOJI DUEL: remember the chicken's 3 emojis, tap them back fastest. Win and the corner is yours forever.
4.Land on your friend: CHICKY-CHICKEN! Chicken dance, back to the middle. Tap the same emoji together to make the chicken laugh.
5.Own all four corners to unlock the rope. Climb 1–3 at a time and land on the star EXACTLY. Beat the final puzzle to win the round.
6.Every round has 6 minutes. When time runs out: FINAL EMOJI DUEL, best of 5. More keys = a head start.
★Every star you win unlocks a new set of doodles. Need a break? The AI button plays your turns for up to 2 minutes.
